2. RIOT GAMES COMPLIANCE & TELEMETRY
- League Stat Tracker complies with the Riot Games Developer Terms and Conditions.

3. VOD RECORDING & MEDIA PRIVACY
- All automated match recordings (MP4/WebM video files) are encoded locally using your PC's hardware encoder (Windows Graphics Capture) and saved directly to your local Videos folder (%USERPROFILE%\Videos\LeagueStatTracker).
